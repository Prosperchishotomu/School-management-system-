1. Project Overview
Build a production‑grade, single‑school management system covering the full Zimbabwean school pathway — ECD A/B, Grades 1–7, Forms 1–4 (O Level), Forms 5–6 (A Level) — configurable to run as a primary‑only, secondary‑only, or combined institution (setting: offered_phases). It must handle admissions, curriculum & subject registration, attendance, timetabling, CA + internal exams, ZIMSEC (or Cambridge) examination entry and results, fees in a dual‑currency environment, boarding (houses, exeat), library, transport, health, guidance & counselling, statutory reporting (EMIS‑style), and communication (SMS/WhatsApp/email).

Standards: OWASP ASVS L2; data integrity via constraints + transactions + append‑only finance ledger; confidentiality per the Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] (POTRAZ‑aligned) with heightened controls for minors' data and examination data.

Default stack: Laravel 11 (PHP 8.3) or NestJS (Node 20) · MySQL 8.0 (InnoDB, utf8mb4) · Redis · React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind (PWA with offline queue for attendance/marks) · S3‑compatible private storage · WebSockets · Docker + Nginx · OpenAPI 3.1.

2. Academic Model (Zimbabwe)
2.1 Calendar
Academic session = calendar year (e.g., 2025).
Three terms per MoPSE calendar, dates entered annually in settings: Term 1 (Jan–Apr), Term 2 (May–Aug), Term 3 (Sep–Dec). All term dates, half‑terms and holidays are editable; MoPSE gazetted dates are seeded as defaults with a note that they change yearly.

2.3 Learning Areas & Subjects
Subjects are configurable, seeded from the ZIMSEC updated‑curriculum learning areas, each tagged with a category, applicable phase(s), core/elective default per phase, and an optional exam board syllabus code (captured by the school from current ZIMSEC/Cambridge syllabus documents — never hard‑coded by the vendor).

Seed learning‑area categories: languages, mathematics, sciences, humanities, commercials, agriculture, technical_vocational, arts, physical_education, general_studies, ict.

Representative seed subjects (school‑editable):

Primary/Infant: English Language; Shona; Ndebele; Mathematics; Science and Technology; Heritage‑Social Studies; FAREME (Family, Religion and Moral Education); Agriculture; Visual and Performing Arts; Physical Education, Sport and Mass Displays; ICT; Guidance and Counselling. (Support any constitutionally recognised Zimbabwean language as a subject: Tonga, Kalanga, Nambya, Sotho, Xhosa, Chewa, Venda, Shangani, Swati, Zulu, Ndau, Chibarwe, Koisan, Sign Language.)
O‑Level (Forms 1–4): English Language; Mathematics; Combined Science; Biology; Chemistry; Physics; Human and Social Biology; Geography; History; Heritage Studies; FAREME; Commerce; Principles of Accounts; Business Studies; Computer Science; Agriculture; Food and Nutrition / Food Technology and Design; Textile Technology and Design; Wood / Metal Technology and Design; Technical Graphics and Design; Art and Design; Music; Physical Education, Sport and Mass Displays; Shona; Ndebele; Literature in English; Literature in Shona; Literature in Ndebele; French; Portuguese; Guidance and Counselling.
A‑Level (Forms 5–6): Mathematics; Physics; Chemistry; Biology; Computer Science; Accounting; Business Studies; Economics; Geography; History; Divinity; Literature in English; Shona; Ndebele; French; Art; Music; General Paper (compulsory subsidiary — enforced by combination rules).
Grade 7 examination subjects (config): English Language, Mathematics, Shona/Ndebele, General Paper.
2.4 Grading Scales & Points (all configurable per school)
ZIMSEC_O — letters A, B, C, D, E, U with points 1,2,3,4,5,6; school‑configurable pass grade (default C) and grade boundaries per subject.
ZIMSEC_A — letters A, B, C, D, E, U with points on the 10‑point scale (A=10, B=8, C=6, D=4, E=2, U=0); alternative 5‑point mapping selectable in settings.
PRIMARY_PCT — percentage with configurable achievement bands/descriptors for Grades 1–7 internal reporting.
ECD_CHECKLIST — descriptive competence scale (e.g., Not yet at expected level / Approaching / At expected level / Above expected level) used for ECD A/B; no ranking or formal exams for ECD.
CAMBRIDGE_* — IGCSE (A*–U or 9–1) and AS/A (A*–U) for schools using Cambridge.
Points rules (settings): o_level_points_best = 6 (sum of best 6 subject points; lower is better), a_level_points_best = 3 (best three A‑Level subjects; higher is better), o_level_mandatory_include (e.g., English Language), a_level_include_general_paper = true. Grade 7 internal reporting uses averages; board results are imported as marks/grades.

2.5 Assessment Architecture (CA + Exams)
Per exam (type: mid_term, end_of_term, mock, continuous, board_sitting) and per class‑subject, define components — e.g., "Tests 20%", "Project/SBA 30%", "Examination 50%" — with weights summing to 100 and max marks each.
Teachers enter marks per component; the system computes the weighted subject mark, maps it to the phase grading scale, assigns grade + points, and derives subject/class/form positions.
Mock exams for Form 4, Form 6 and Grade 7 mirror board structure.
ZIMSEC School‑Based Assessment (SBA/CA) marks for relevant syllabi can be flagged and exported separately (Section 5.14).
Report card formats per phase: ECD checklist (no rank); Primary (marks %, position, remarks); O‑Level (%, grade A–U, points); A‑Level (%, grade, points). Every report card includes attendance summary, conduct, class teacher remark, and Head's remark with a signature/approval workflow.
2.6 Promotion Rules (configurable engine)
Grade 1–6 / Form 1–3: internal results thresholds per promotion_rules.
Grade 7 → Form 1: internal results + board results where received; supports acceptance/placement workflow.
Form 4 → Form 5: requires configured O‑Level passes (default: ≥5 subjects at grade C or better including English Language; sciences combinations may require Maths/Science — configurable).
Form 5 → Form 6: internal promotion thresholds.
Repeating a level ("repeat") must be a first‑class flow, not a delete/re‑create.

4. API Conventions
Unchanged from standard best practice, with these specifics:

Base: https://<domain>/api/v1/...; URL versioning; Sunset header on deprecations.
Auth: Bearer JWT (RS256, 15 min; claims sub, roles, sid, jti); refresh token = opaque 256‑bit, stored hashed, 7‑day sliding, rotated with reuse detection, Secure; HttpOnly; SameSite=Strict cookie.
Public IDs: UUIDs in URLs; integer PKs never exposed.
List params: ?page=&per_page≤100&sort=&q=&filter[...]=.
Success envelope: {data, meta:{page, per_page, total, request_id}} · Error envelope: {error:{code, message, fields}, request_id}.
Headers: X-Request-ID, Idempotency-Key (required on payments, invoice generation, entry submission, results import), Accept-Language: en|sn|nd (UI strings; subject names may exist in local languages).
Offline sync: bulk attendance/marks endpoints are idempotent upserts accepting client‑generated UUIDs + synced_at; PWA queues when offline and replays on reconnect (server validates windows and conflicts with 409 STALE_VERSION).
Rate limits: auth/OTP 5/min per IP+account (exponential lockout); general 120/min/user; uploads 10/min → 429 + Retry-After.
Status codes: 200/201/204 · 400 · 401 · 403 · 404 · 409 conflict/stale · 422 business rule · 429 · 500.
Error codes (additions to standard set): RESULTS_NOT_RELEASED, ENTRY_CLOSED, COMBINATION_INVALID, SBA_REQUIRED, DUPLICATE_CANDIDATE, FX_RATE_MISSING, APPROVAL_REQUIRED, MAKER_CHECKER_VIOLATION.

5. Database Schema
Global conventions: id BIGINT PK; uuid CHAR(36) UNIQUE on externally visible tables; created_at/updated_at/deleted_at (soft delete on business tables); created_by/updated_by FK→users; FKs ON DELETE RESTRICT unless noted; InnoDB/utf8mb4; 🔒 = field‑level AES‑256‑GCM encryption (keys in vault/KMS).

8. Security Requirements
All v1 requirements apply (TLS 1.2+/1.3 + HSTS; Argon2id; MFA mandatory for SA/HM/BU/AD; refresh‑rotation with reuse detection; brute‑force lockouts; AES‑256‑GCM field encryption for 🔒 columns with vault‑held keys; parameterized queries + CSP + CSRF + SameSite=Strict; upload whitelist/AV/private buckets; deny‑by‑default RBAC + row‑level policies; UUIDs + 404‑on‑foreign‑resources; append‑only audit with least‑privilege DB grants; dependency scanning + security headers; automated security tests as a deploy gate). Zimbabwe‑specific additions:

Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] (POTRAZ): document the school as data controller; appoint a DPO (configurable contact surfaced in privacy pages); lawful basis + guardian consent captured at admission for minors; data subject access/erasure endpoints (§6.15); documented breach procedure with notification workflow; cross‑border transfer safeguards (prefer in‑country/regional hosting; encrypt backups wherever stored).
Examination data confidentiality: ZIMSEC entries, candidate numbers, SBA marks, and imported board results are classified confidential — access logged, bulk exports require maker‑checker approval, exports are watermarked, and no third‑party analytics may process them.
Results embargo: entry_results and published report cards are hidden from ST/PA until release_at; server returns RESULTS_NOT_RELEASED; PDF stamps are watermarked with holder name and timestamp.
Maker‑checker: results publish/release, entry submission, results import, payment reversal, invoice voiding, fee‑structure activation require two distinct users; approvals rows are immutable and audited.
PII hygiene: no PII in logs/URLs/analytics; masking in all list endpoints (full values on detail view only, with audit); Zimbabwean national IDs and birth certificate entries treated as sensitive identifiers.
9. Data Integrity Requirements
All v1 rules apply (constraints first; transactions around enrollment/promotion/invoice generation/payment posting/result publishing/admission conversion; optimistic locking with 409 STALE_VERSION; idempotency keys; referential safety with soft delete; nightly consistency jobs; whitelisted state machines). Additions:

Multi‑currency ledger: every payment stores currency_id, fx_rate, and amount_base at transaction time; FX rows are immutable; nightly reconciliation recomputes base‑currency totals and alerts on drift (never auto‑mutates).
Component weights: server rejects exam activation unless each (exam, class‑subject) has components summing to exactly 100; weighted totals computed server‑side only; publish is idempotent and freezes results.
Points/grade computation: pure, unit‑tested functions per scale (O‑Level best‑6 with mandatory‑include rules; A‑Level best‑3 + General Paper; deterministic tie‑breaking for ranks — config: sum of best marks, then alphabetical).
Board results import: file hash stored; dry‑run mode reports row‑level errors (unknown candidate/subject, duplicate, invalid grade); commit is all‑or‑nothing per import batch; duplicates blocked by UNIQUE constraints; import is reversible only via compensating rows, never silent edits.
Candidate numbers: unique per entry; immutable after submission; any change requires a new revision row.
Exeat↔attendance consistency: a student checked out cannot be marked present for that period; nightly job flags conflicts for the boarding office.
Fee structures: versioned (retired rows retained); invoices copy item amounts at generation time so later edits never mutate issued invoices.
10. Compliance & Statutory Reporting (Zimbabwe)
MoPSE/EMIS: one‑click generation of the core returns — enrolment by phase/grade/sex (ECD A → Upper 6, including repeaters), staff establishment (posts, qualifications, subject specialisations) — as CSV/PDF; snapshot + submission tracking in statutory_returns.
Statutory registers: printable Admission Register (chronological, permanent) and daily Attendance Registers per class/month with signature column.
Teacher professional requirements: schemes of work and lesson plans per class‑subject‑term with HOD approval and record‑of‑work capture; inspection‑ready export pack per teacher/department.
Discipline: no corporal punishment anywhere in the data model or UI; suspension/expulsion workflows reference MoPSE procedures; counselling referrals recorded and tracked by GC.
Governance: SDA levy line items labelled as such; fee‑structure activation recorded with approver (supports SDC/SDA consultation records).
Retention: pupil academic records & admission register permanent; audit logs 7 years; login attempts 90 days; counselling notes 5 years post‑leaving; rejected applications 2 years; board entry files 7 years.
11. Non‑Functional Requirements (Zimbabwe context)
Performance: p95 < 300 ms reads / < 500 ms writes; Redis caching for dashboards/timetables; queued jobs for SMS/email/PDF/imports with retries + alerts.
Offline-first PWA: attendance and marks entry work offline (IndexedDB queue + idempotent replay); explicit sync status UI; conflict surfacing.
Low bandwidth: gzip/brotli, paginated payloads, image compression, reduced‑payload mode.
Messaging: SMS as the primary parent channel (pluggable gateway adapter — e.g., local aggregators or Twilio/Africa's Talking), email secondary, WhatsApp optional; per‑event channel matrix in settings.
Payments: gateway adapter interface with reference implementations for Paynow and EcoCash merchant flows; all others via manual capture + bank‑statement reconciliation.
Backups/DR: encrypted daily full + hourly binlog, 30‑day retention, quarterly restore drills; hosting in‑country or regional with documented cross‑transfer safeguards.
Observability: structured JSON logs with request IDs; Sentry; queue/DB dashboards; uptime checks.
i18n: UI locales en, sn, nd; timezone Africa/Harati? — Africa/Harare; date format DD/MM/YYYY; number/currency formatting per locale.
CI/CD: lint → unit (≥80% services; points/FX/weighted‑marks functions at 100%) → integration (≥70%) → e2e happy paths → migration dry‑run → deploy behind flags.
